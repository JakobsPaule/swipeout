Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

👆

Swipe to decide

Swipe left to mark a photo for deletion, right to keep it. Tap buttons if you prefer. Undo any decision instantly.

🗂

Browse modes

Newest first, oldest first, random shuffle, or browse by album — including smart albums.

✅

Review before deleting

Photos are queued — never deleted on swipe. Review the list, then confirm in one tap.

🛡

Safe by design

Deletion moves photos to iOS Recently Deleted — you have 30 days to recover anything by mistake.

📊

Stats

See how many photos you've deleted and how much storage you've freed — session and lifetime totals.

🔒

100% private

No accounts, no analytics, no uploads. Everything happens on your device.
