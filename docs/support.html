Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos permanently deleted when I swipe left?

No. Swiping left only adds a photo to a review queue inside the app. Nothing is deleted until you tap Confirm Deletion — and even then, photos are sent to iOS "Recently Deleted", where they remain for 30 days before being permanently erased. You can recover them anytime from the iOS Photos app during that window.

Does Library Control upload my photos anywhere?

Never. All processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's PhotoKit framework. The app makes no network requests and has no analytics. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Why does Library Control only show some of my photos?

You may have granted Limited Access rather than Full Access when the app first asked for permission. To allow access to your full library, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Library Control and select Full Access. You can also tap Manage in Settings from the banner inside the app.

Can I undo a swipe?

Yes — tap the Undo button (curved arrow) at any point during a session to reverse your last decision, including deletions and favorites.

What does "Favorite" (swipe up) do?

Swiping up is a "super like" — it keeps the photo and marks it as a Favorite in your iOS Photos library, so it appears in the built-in Favorites smart album.

I accidentally cleared my review history. Will I see all photos again?

Yes. Clearing review history removes the record of which photos you've already swiped on. The next session will present all photos again from the beginning.

How do I reset my stats?

Go to Settings → Reset Lifetime Stats. This clears the counters only — it does not affect your photos or review history.
