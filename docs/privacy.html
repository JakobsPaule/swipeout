Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2025

Short version: SwipeClean does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and never leave it.

1. Who we are

SwipeClean is an iOS application developed by Jakob Paul ("we", "us", or "our"). You can reach us at paul_jakob@icloud.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information.
  • We do not access, store, or transmit your photos or any photo metadata to any server.
  • We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry SDKs.
  • We do not use advertising networks or tracking libraries.
  • We do not require you to create an account.

3. Photo library access

SwipeClean requests permission to access your photo library solely to display photos within the app so you can review and delete them. This access is:

  • Processed entirely on your device using Apple's PhotoKit framework.
  • Never transmitted to any server or third party.
  • Used only while the app is open and in use.

You may revoke photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

4. Local data storage

SwipeClean stores the following data locally on your device using UserDefaults:

  • Lifetime statistics: the number of photos you have deleted and the estimated storage freed.
  • App preferences such as whether you have completed the onboarding tour.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Third-party services

SwipeClean does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud services. The app makes no network requests.

6. Children's privacy

SwipeClean does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Because we collect no data from anyone, the app is safe for all ages.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the app, please contact us:
